How the aircraft finds itself without GPS

GNSS (GPS) can be jammed. This flight replays a simulated fixed-wing UAV whose GNSS is switched off mid-flight; the only position information left comes from its downward-looking camera.

1Camera ↔ satellite mapA neural network (DINOv2, fine-tuned) finds where the camera image is on a satellite map; feature matching (LightGlue) pins it to a few meters.
2Sanity check → autopilotThe fix is checked against the barometer and IMU, then sent to the ArduPilot autopilot as if it came from a GPS receiver.
3Odometry in betweenOver water, uniform fields or in turns the map match can fail. Frame-to-frame motion then carries the position until the next match.
4Targets on the groundVehicles are detected (YOLO), placed on the map with the camera pose of the same frame, and tracked — moving ones get a speed and heading.

Compare with a control flight (no visual navigation): the autopilot drifts hundreds of meters. Click the error chart at the bottom to jump in time, space = play/pause, 3D VIEW to watch the aircraft from outside.
